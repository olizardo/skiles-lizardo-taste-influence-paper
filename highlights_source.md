**Stay, React, or Conform? Examining Social Influence Effects on Aesthetic Judgments**

Omar Lizardo & Sara Skiles — *Poetics* (POETICS-D-26-00490, Revision 1)

- Survey experiment tests how others' views causally shape individual taste judgments
- Seeing an artwork a second time raises liking it, even with no feedback from others
- News that others dislike an artwork erases this boost; praise adds nothing extra
- Highly educated people devalue an artwork after learning working-class fans like it
- People whose education and class identity diverge conform most to others' tastes
